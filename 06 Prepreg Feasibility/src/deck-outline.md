## Slide 1: Title

- Prepreg Feasibility: SN6 Wing Endplates
- FEB Composites · PFS-001 Rev A · 2026-07-31
- Goal line: "SN5 endplates were 2272 g of flat plate. Here is the plan to fix that."

## Slide 2: The problem

- SN5 RW endplates (P-SN5-008): 2272 g, glass infusion over 0.25 in Nomex, 7 plies (WO-SN5-008).
- Flat plates should not weigh this much.
- Why they came out heavy: glass needs ~3x the plies of carbon for the same stiffness, and infusion resin content is whatever the bag decides.

## Slide 3: The proposal

- Prepreg carbon skins, cocured to our donated Nomex core, vacuum bag only, oven cure.
- Prepreg = cloth with factory-set resin, cures with heat. No mixing, no pot life, no infusion lines.
- Estimated 35 to 45 percent panel weight saving (measured panels supersede estimates).

## Slide 4: Two material paths

- Path A: ACP RTS prepreg. 198 gsm 2x2 twill. Room-temp storage, no freezer. Cure 4 h at 132 °C. Our existing sponsor sells expired rolls at clearance.
- Path B: XPREG XC110. 210 gsm 2x2 twill. Cure from 85 °C (DIY-oven friendly). Needs freezer, 30-day out-life, UK shipping (customs risk PP-02).
- The coupling: A deletes the freezer but raises the oven bar; B lowers the oven bar but needs the freezer.
- Decision Sep 4, after ACP responds and the oven question is answered.

## Slide 5: Will it bond to honeycomb without an autoclave?

- Yes, per published VBO studies. Known process, known controls.
- Our advantage: endplates are flat with square-cut core. The classic core-crush driver (ramped edges) is absent.
- Fillets come from XA120 adhesive film (or self-adhesive prepreg if the coupon proves it).
- Controls: partially vented core per literature, section cuts on Panel 2, drop test carried over from CS-006.

## Slide 6: What we need from RFS (the Larry conversation)

- Oven options if none exists: dedicated used domestic oven ($50 to 150) or DIY PID box ($200 to 400).

## Slide 10: Go/no-go criteria

- At least 30 percent lighter per m² than the glass baseline (measured).
- Bend stiffness at or above the glass panel.
- No core crush; section cuts show continuous fillets.
- Every cure log inside the TDS band.
- Out-of-pocket at or under $500.
- "We decide with a scale and a bend test, not vibes."
