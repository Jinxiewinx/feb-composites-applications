About

A student team that ships a car, not a plan for one.

Founded 2019. Every fall we recruit, every winter we build, every June we race at FSAE Electric — then write down everything that went wrong so the next class starts smarter.

Layups, machining, harness. Coffee consumption: elevated.
Apr–May
Test
Shakedown, tuning, driver training. Coffee: do not ask.
June
Compete
FSAE EV, Michigan. Everything else was for this.
